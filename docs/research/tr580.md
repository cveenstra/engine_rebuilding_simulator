# TR580 research notes

Unit file: `src/data/units/tr580.json` (114 steps, 31 specs, 21 sources).
Vehicle: 2014–2018 Subaru Forester 2.5i (FB25), Forester SJ.

## Which TR580 this is

"TR580" covers several generations. The Forester SJ uses the **second-generation** unit (Gen 2), the same
family as 2012+ Impreza/XV and the FB25 Legacy/Outback (s16). That matters because the public rebuild
literature splits by generation:

- **Gen 1** (2010–2012 Legacy/Outback 2.5 with EJ253). The ATRA 2015 webinar handout describes this one: a different
  layout with an intermediate case, a seven-solenoid valve body in the pan, and a chain removed by pulling
  the secondary pulley open with a puller. **Not used for any step or spec.** It's listed below only so
  nobody copies its numbers in later.
- **Gen 2** (Forester SJ). The forward clutch rides on the input shaft ahead of the primary pulley, the valve body sits on top of
  the case with six solenoids, the oil pump is chain-driven off the converter hub, and the chain comes off by
  *pushing* the secondary pulley open (s9, s10, s11, s13). Every step follows this layout.
- Later TR580 (2017+ Impreza, 2019+ Forester SK) is a different evolution. Its bulletins are only used where
  they explicitly list the 2014–2018 Forester or the TR580 in general.

### Consulted but not used

- **Copies of the Subaru factory service manual** on third-party sites (ddddesigns.com, jdmfsm.info) turned up in search.
  They weren't used and nothing in the unit comes from them. They're unlicensed copies of paywalled STIS material.
  STIS itself (techinfo.subaru.com) needs a subscription and wasn't reachable.
- **subaru-specs.com** "TR580 specifications" page: it gives 4-speed gear ratios and Subaru ATF-HP, which is wrong for a CVT. Rejected.
- **ATRA "Subaru Lineartronic CVT Rebuild" webinar handout (2015)**,
  https://atracom.blob.core.windows.net/webinars/import/subaru_lineartronic_cvt_rebuild.pdf. This is Gen 1. Its
  clearances (forward 1.2–1.6 mm, reverse piston stroke 2.9–3.2 mm, "input clutch" 2.4–2.8 mm, transfer
  0.70–1.1 mm, replace above 1.6 mm) and its seven-solenoid list don't apply to the Forester's Gen 2.
- **TransTec "TR580 Subaru CVT" teardown video** (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yR2Y9gS_3xM). Only the
  description could be read, so it was dropped from the unit's sources.
- **Forum threads on subaruforester.org and subaruoutback.org** (valve body DIY, front diff oil change, Gen 2 teardown)
  redirect to a paid crawler gate (tollbit). Not fetched.
- **go-parts.com** claims the 2017 Forester TCM is built into the valve body. Not used. See open questions.

## Conflicts

- **Overhaul fill volume.** AKPPro gives 11.9–12.4 L and oils-finder 12.4 L. A search-engine summary of owner's-manual
  quotes gave 12.2 L (12.9 US qt) for non-turbo, but it wasn't read first-hand. The step gives "roughly 12 L" and leans on the level check.
- **Front diff oil.** AKPPro gives 1.35 L and oils-finder 1.4 L. Both are shown as a range.
- **Fluid name across 2014–2018.** Recommended Materials (s3) lists only CVTF II for the NA TR580. GEARS (s12) says the earlier blue CVTF
  was discontinued around 2015 and CVTF II took over. Early-2014 Foresters were probably factory-filled with the
  earlier fluid. The supersession is implied but no single document states it for this car.
- **Reverse brake selective part.** One read of GEARS Part 2 says the reverse adjustment uses a selective pressure
  plate; the article is explicit only that the forward *apply plate* comes in thicknesses. The step just says "selective plate".

## Steps that rest on general rebuild practice, not a TR580-specific source

These are standard transmission practice. They're in the unit because leaving them out would teach the wrong order,
but no source above documents them for this unit:

- Soaking new frictions in CVTF before stacking (folded into steps 59 and 61).
- Marking the chain's running direction before removal and reinstalling a reused chain the same way (31, 74).
- Turning the converter through each spline engagement to confirm it's fully seated (98). s1 only says to reinstall it carefully.
- Drawing the CVT to the engine with the housing bolts, never the converter nuts (99).
- Flushing the cooler circuit before reconnecting (101).
- Setting the inhibitor switch in neutral (91).
- Re-setting pinion depth and backlash if the hypoid set, bearings or case are replaced (67). s10 covers only the pinion support's bolts and seals.
- Re-measuring pulley and transfer shims if bearings or cases are replaced (72, 83). The shims' existence is sourced; the selection method isn't.

## Open questions

1. **TCM location on the 2014–2018 Forester.** It looks like a separate module: 16-111-18 gives TCM part numbers
   30919AF83x/84x, and 16-131-20R splits vehicles into "TCM in cabin/engine bay" and "TCM inside the transmission".
   Not confirmed, so the unit treats the TCM only through scan-tool work and never as something removed with the bench unit.
2. **Pulley shim selection.** How the primary front bearing and secondary rear shims are measured and chosen, and the target values.
3. **Forward/reverse unit end play.** The Gen 1 handout has a forward drum end-play shim. Whether Gen 2 has one isn't documented publicly.
4. **Torque values** for everything except the chain cover and filler plug.
5. **Secondary pulley disassembly** on Gen 2 (spring, piston seals). Left out. The Gen 1 handout warns the spring is very strong and uses a press.
6. **Factory expander tool number.** Search results attribute 18769AA010 "Expander Pulley" to the secondary pulley tool, but no
   page with that number could be read, so the unit doesn't print it.
7. **Torque converter relearn procedure.** s5 requires it but the steps live in the service manual, so the unit names it and doesn't describe it.

## Least-certain items in the unit

1. Clutch clearances (forward 1.10–1.40, reverse 2.30–2.70, transfer 0.7–1.1 mm) come from one aftermarket Gen 2 teardown, not factory text.
2. The pulley bearing step (s20) comes from a search summary of a 403'd forum thread.
3. The teardown order between the top valve body, extension case, pan and front pump drive. Sources agree on the internal order but
   differ slightly on the external order. The unit follows the I Do Cars sequence (s14) and the GEARS bolt counts.
4. One-time-use axle seals and C-clips (s6) come from a Gen 1 bulletin, carried over to Gen 2.
5. Stall speeds come from the 2017 Forester campaign and are assumed for 2014–2016 and 2018.
