# FB25 research notes

Unit file: `src/data/units/fb25.json` (183 steps, 85 specs, 16 sources).
Vehicle: 2014–2018 Subaru Forester 2.5i (Forester SJ), engine FB25B, paired with the TR580 CVT.

All step prose was written fresh. The sources below supplied facts only (values, part names, tool numbers, order of work), never wording.

## Which FB25 this is

- **Engine code.** FB25B, used in the 2011-2018 North American Forester and 2013-2019 Legacy/Outback (s14). The 2019+ Forester moved to the direct-injected FB25D, which is out of scope.
- **Primary source is the exact vehicle.** The main source (s1 to s4) is the 2014 Forester service manual, engine sections for the FB25 (Subaru labels it H4DO in the Forester book). Subaru's own STIS (techinfo.subaru.com) needs a subscription and was not reachable.
- **Cross-check source.** The 2015-2019 Legacy BN/BS manual (s5) covers the later FB25 used in Legacy/Outback. It matches the Forester manual on procedure and on most torque values, but several limits differ (see Conflicts). Where they differ, the 2014 Forester numbers were used.
- **No piston pin service holes.** The prompt asked for "pistons and pins through the service holes". That is the EJ procedure. The FB25 manual (s1) removes each piston and rod through its bore after the heads and oil pan upper are off, with caps reached from the oil pan side, and only then splits the block. Reassembly is the reverse: crank and block halves first, then pistons and rods down the bores, caps fitted from below, final rod bolt angle with temporary bolts as angle-gauge anchors. The diagonally split rods (s13) are what make this possible. The steps follow the FB order.

## Text extraction caveats

The Forester PDFs are "Print to PDF" files whose embedded font drops the letter "t" and the "±" sign in extracted text ("0.220.02" means 0.22 ± 0.02; "1324" is the 1-3-2-4 firing order; "2.498" is 2,498 cc). Every value used was re-read in context and, where possible, compared against the Legacy manual's clean HTML. Figures (bolt sequence diagrams, ring gap positions, pry points) are images and could not be read, so steps say "in the factory sequence" rather than listing bolt numbers.

## Open questions and things not confirmed

1. **2015-2018 running changes.** All numbers come from the 2014 book. The Legacy comparison shows Subaru changed rings, cams, pistons and some limits for 2015 on the Legacy/Outback. Whether and when the Forester got the same changes (2017 is the likely year) is not confirmed. The unit's first note tells the learner to check STIS by VIN.
2. **Head bolt and joining bolt reuse.** Neither manual marks head bolts or block joining bolts as one-time use (rod cap bolts are explicitly new). Many builders replace head bolts anyway. Left unstated in the steps.
3. **Drive plate bolt torque** is in the CVT(TR580) section, which was not downloaded. The step says so in its spec note rather than giving a number.
4. **EGR pipe torques.** The figure assigns 6.4 and 51 N·m to two fasteners but the image is not readable, so no spec was given.
5. **Ring gap clocking.** The manual gives the second ring 180 deg from the top ring, expander 180 deg from the upper rail and lower rail 120 deg clockwise from the upper rail, but the starting positions of the top ring and upper rail are only in a figure. The spec says so.
6. **Oil priming.** The Subaru manual has no priming step. The prime-and-first-run steps rely on a Subaru performance builder (s12, vendor) and on the manual's own fuel-pump-fuse method from the compression test.
7. **Forums.** NASIOC, SubaruForester.org (tollbit redirect), BobIsTheOilGuy and GRM all returned 403 to automated fetches, so no forum thread was read directly. Common-mistake text comes from Subaru bulletins (s6 to s9) and s15 instead.
8. **Videos.** No FB25 rebuild video could be located and verified, so none is cited.

## Deliberately left out

- Hood, grille and damper repositioning, select-monitor relearns, idle and ignition timing checks, TGV and injector electrical tests: not rebuild steps.
- Valve spring load values, valve head diameters and valve lengths: measured in the spring test step but only the free length is given as the spec, to keep one number per step.
- Sensor torques for the cam position sensors, oil temperature sensor and oil pressure switch: in other manual sections not checked.
- The turbo FA20DIT (H4DOTC) material that shares the Forester PDFs.
- A separate "wash block after machining" step was drafted and removed because no source in hand states it.
